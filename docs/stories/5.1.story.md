### Story
**As a** user,
**I want** to see a tabbed interface for downloading or ordering my certificate,
**so that** I can easily choose my desired option.

### Acceptance Criteria
1. The `OtpVerificationModal` is removed from the `GeneratePage`.
2. The `CertificateDisplay` component is removed, and its preview logic is integrated into `GeneratePage`.
3. A `shadcn/ui` Tabs component is added to `GeneratePage` with two tabs: "Download E-certificate" and "Order Hard Copy".
4. Placeholder components `EcertificateTab.tsx` and `HardCopyTab.tsx` are created and rendered within the corresponding tab panels.
